## 👨‍💻 About Me

I'm an Ethiopian creative technologist and IT graduate passionate about building meaningful digital experiences through design, technology, and storytelling.

I work across graphic design, brand identity, web development, digital content creation, and social media management. My goal is to bridge the gap between creative ideas and practical technology.

* 🎨 Founder of **[Amen Creative](https://amencreativedesigns.com)** — a creative brand established in 2019.
* 💻 IT graduate from Haramaya University.
* 🚀 Building **Skill Mesob**, an e-learning platform focused on expanding access to digital learning.
* 🖥️ Interested in full-stack development, UI/UX, and digital products.
* 📸 Creating visual content and digital experiences for brands.
* 🌍 Based in Ethiopia, building for a connected world.

> I believe great design attracts attention, great technology creates possibilities, and great ideas bring them together.
